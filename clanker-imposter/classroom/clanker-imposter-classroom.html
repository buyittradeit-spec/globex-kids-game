📖 How to play
🏫 Your school is hidden inside a giant Clanker warehouse. One of the kids is secretly a robot in disguise, and it can shapeshift into anyone!
📓 Find the math notebooks and solve them. Get one wrong and the Clanker hears you... CLANK... CLANK... RUN!
🧊 The Clanker zaps kids into ice statues. Find one? Hit REPORT for an emergency meeting and vote! Or press the 🔴 red button in the lunchroom (once per game).
🧑‍🏫 Teachers patrol the hallways. No hall pass? Get one from the Principal's Office!
🔧 The Clanker can sabotage the school: lights, intercom, boiler, air filters, doors, costumes. Fix it fast!
🤓 Theo the Teacher's Pet gives pop quizzes in the halls: 5 answers before time runs out or he tells the Principal!
🎒 Grab items you find (carry 3; use with 1 2 3). Watch out for Big Brock the bully! Tell a teacher if he bothers you.
🏆 Kids win by solving ALL notebooks or voting out the Clanker. 🤖 The Clanker wins if only 1 kid is left.
🎮 Game controllers work too! (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro, USB; 2 controllers for split screen)
🎮 3D view: W/S or ↑/↓ walk, A/D step sideways, ←/→ or drag the mouse to look. Phone: left thumb walks, right thumb looks.

ℹ️ About Clanker Imposter

Clanker Imposter is a Globex Kids game, designed by kids. Find the robots hiding in your school, solve math notebooks, and vote the Clankers out!

🔒 Privacy (for parents & teachers)

  • No data collected. No accounts, no names, no emails, no tracking.
  • No ads and no in-game purchases.
  • Works offline. This Classroom Edition never connects to the internet; it is one file that runs in any web browser.
  • No chat with strangers. There is no online play; everyone else in the school is a computer character.
  • Game settings (volume, number of kids, etc.) are saved only in this browser on this device and never leave it.
  • The optional singing voice uses the device's built-in speech voice.
