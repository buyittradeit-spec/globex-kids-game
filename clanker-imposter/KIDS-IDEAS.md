# Clanker Imposter: Game Design (by the kids!)

## Name
**Clanker Imposter** (picked by the kids)

## The idea
Kids explore their school doing math notebooks. One kid is secretly a robot (the Clanker Imposter) in disguise. Find it before it freezes everyone!

## The map
A **school built INSIDE a giant Clanker warehouse** 🏭: classrooms and a lunchroom surrounded by robot factory stuff.

**Rooms:** Lunchroom (emergency meetings happen here), Classroom, Gym, Library, Bathroom.

## Players
- **Kids** walk around the school finding and solving math notebooks.
- **The Clanker Imposter** is a robot disguised as a kid.
  - Has a **shapeshift button**: can turn into ANY kid to trick everyone.
  - **No visual clues!** The disguise is perfect. Kids catch the robot by how it acts: who was near the frozen kid, who never does notebooks, clanky footsteps.

## Math notebooks (easy, about 3rd grade)
- Addition (7 + 5)
- Subtraction (12 − 4)
- Multiplication (3 × 4)
- Division (12 ÷ 3)
- **Right answer:** notebook done ⭐
- **Wrong answer:** the robot **targets you**. You hear *clank... clank... CLANK* getting louder as it gets closer. Run!

## Freeze ray
- The robot zaps kids with a freeze ray and they turn into a **frozen ice statue** 🧊.
- Frozen kids float around as ghosts and can still finish notebooks.

## Report + emergency meeting
- A kid who finds a frozen ice statue hits **REPORT**.
- The lunch bell rings 🔔 and everyone goes to an **emergency meeting** in the lunchroom.
- Everyone votes on who they think the robot is.

## Robot giveaways (when faking lunch / acting normal)
- Bites the tray instead of the pizza
- Drinks milk and it spills out of its ears

## When the Clanker gets caught
It **runs in circles**, faster and faster, until its **head pops off** 💥 and bounces across the room.

## Inspired by (but we make our OWN look, characters and names)
- School-and-notebooks style games
- Imposter / vote-someone-out style games

## How to win
- **Kids win** if they finish ALL the math notebooks OR vote out the Clanker.
- **The Clanker wins** when it has frozen everyone until only 1 kid is left.
